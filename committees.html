Committees

Anyone interested in serving on a committee should contact the chair. To chair a committee, contact President Greg Tennyson. Chairs are appointed in January. Contact chairs through the Local office unless otherwise noted.

Audit Committee

Chair: Kwami Barnes
Required by the CWA Constitution to review the Local's financial statements and records.

Building Committee

Chair: Greg Tennyson

Community Services Committee

Chair: Mira Radulovic
Assists members and their families receiving services available from the Local and the community.

Editors Committee (Wiretap)

Chair: Rich Hinderholtz
Responsible for the layout, printing, and distribution of the Local Newsletter – The Wiretap. Submit items to wiretap@cwa4603.com.

Education Committee

Chair: Dru Zellmer
Assists officers in training and education of stewards and members.

Election Committee

Chair: Carrie Dercola
Handles all elections of the Local, including absentee ballots and coverage of polling locations.

Women’s / Equity Committee

Chair: Shawne Stephens
Ensures involvement of the Local in affairs pertaining to women and ethnic groups within the company and the community.

Finance Committee

Chair: Kwami Barnes
Examines and helps guide the Local's financial position and prepares an Annual Financial Budget.

Legislative Action Committee

Chair: Louria Jewett
Active in voter registration programs and advises the membership on pending legislation relative to members.

Mobilization Committee

Chair: Carrie Dercola
Responsible for developing and coordinating activities for mobilizing the membership.

CWA Strong / Next Generation

Chair: Louria Jewett
Develops and mentors the next generation of leaders within the union.

Occupational Training Committee

Chair: Greg Tennyson
Conducts appropriate training sessions to prepare members for job qualification tests.

Organizing Committee

Chair: Greg Tennyson
Assists with organizing non-union employees within the Local's jurisdiction.

Recreation Committee

Chair: Josh Bailey
Responsible for all athletic programs of the Local.

Safety Committee

Chair: Dru Zellmer
Keeps the membership informed on current regulations concerning safety requirements of federal and state government agencies.

Special Events Committee

Chair: Mira Radulovic
Plans all of the Local's social functions.

Work Protection Committee

Chair: Greg Tennyson
Charged with regaining traditional work lost to outside contractors and preventing erosion of future work.
